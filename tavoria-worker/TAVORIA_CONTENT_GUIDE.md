# Tavoria Content Guide

Use this guide for social posts, reels, carousels, screenshots, and new UI copy. The goal is a clear editorial look: warm paper, dark ink, one orange accent, and generous space.

## Brand palette

| Use | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper background | Warm off-white | `#F7F4EE` |
| Secondary paper | Soft warm grey | `#F1EFE8` |
| Main text | Deep navy | `#0E1A24` |
| Body text | Ink | `#263542` |
| Muted text | Slate grey | `#626B78` |
| Accent | Tavoria orange | `#F0531C` |
| Accent wash | Soft orange | `#FFF0E7` |
| White surface | White | `#FFFFFF` |

Use orange for a short keyword, first letter, dot, arrow, status, or primary action. Do not use orange as the background for whole layouts unless the format is intentionally a high-energy announcement.

## Typography

- **Instrument Serif Regular**: headlines, hooks, page titles, and short statements.
- **Hanken Grotesk**: body copy, buttons, navigation, metadata, and captions.
- **DM Mono Medium**: small labels, categories, dates, counters, and status text.

Rules:

- Use sentence case. Avoid all caps except for short DM Mono labels.
- Use one strong headline, then supporting copy in Hanken Grotesk.
- Keep headlines short enough to scan in one glance.
- Use normal letter spacing. Do not tighten type to force a line to fit.
- Keep muted text visibly secondary, never as the main message.

## Chips and pills

Use the shared `Chip` component for app UI. The standard chip is a white pill with dark text, a quiet border, and generous horizontal padding.

- Regular: minimum height `40px`, horizontal padding `16px`.
- Compact: minimum height `36px`, horizontal padding `13px`.
- Gap between chips: `8px`.
- Neutral: white background, dark text, subtle border.
- Selected: dark navy with white text.
- Brand-selected: soft orange with orange text and border.
- Let chips wrap or scroll horizontally. Never clip their labels.
- Keep labels concise: role, city, contract type, pay, or one clear status.

## Reels and carousels

- Canvas: `1080 x 1920`, 9:16.
- Keep important text inside a `96px` horizontal safe margin and away from the top and bottom UI areas.
- Use warm paper for explanatory or candidate content.
- Use dark navy for urgent venue content or contrast between posts.
- Use orange only as the signal, not as decoration everywhere.
- One idea per frame. Prefer 3 to 8 words for the main hook.
- Use 3 to 5 carousel slides: hook, problem, simple explanation, action.
- Keep the same type scale, margins, and alignment across every slide.
- Prefer real venue or product media. Use a graphic treatment when the message is the subject.

Suggested scale for a 1080px reel:

- Hook: Instrument Serif, `88-120px`.
- Supporting line: Hanken Grotesk, `34-46px`.
- Small label: DM Mono, `20-26px`, uppercase.
- Footer or CTA: Hanken Grotesk semibold, `28-36px`.

## Copy style

- Be direct: say what the person can do next.
- Prefer concrete verbs: `Trova`, `Pubblica`, `Candidati`, `Assumi`.
- Avoid paragraphs in visual content.
- Avoid unexplained product language and generic claims.
- Keep Italian punctuation and capitalization consistent with the app.

## Final checklist

- Is there one clear message?
- Does the headline work without the supporting copy?
- Is the orange accent doing a job?
- Can every label be read on a phone?
- Are spacing, type, and alignment consistent across the set?
- Does the final frame say what to do next?
